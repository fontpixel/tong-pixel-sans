通格像素黑体Tong Pixel Sans

14 像素的泛中日韩点阵黑体。同一个字，简体、繁体、日文、韩文各有各的写法；这套字体把四种写法都画成了像素。 A 14-pixel pan-CJK bitmap sans. The same character is written one way in Simplified Chinese, another in Traditional Chinese, Japanese or Korean, and this font draws each region’s form in pixels.

目前 1,249 个字形已由作者逐个审核通过，其余是 AI 修整后待审的版本。欢迎试用、欢迎指错。 1,249 glyphs have been reviewed and approved by the author one by one; the rest are AI-repaired and awaiting review. Try it and point out what’s wrong.

同一段话，四种写法One text, four forms

字格The cell

每个字都在 14 × 14 的格子里。汉字墨迹 13 × 13，左边和底下各留一格；上升 11 像素，下降 3 像素。西文字母按比例宽度排，等宽版里是 7 像素。Every glyph sits in a 14 × 14 cell. Han ink is 13 × 13 with one blank column on the left and one blank row at the bottom; ascent 11 px, descent 3 px. Latin letters are proportional, or 7 px wide in the mono faces.

怎么做的How it is made

这套字不是一个人一笔一笔画出来的，也还没有画完。说清楚它是怎么来的：This font was not drawn stroke by stroke by one person, and it is not finished. Here is how it came about:

  1. 点阵化Rasterise

    从思源黑体（Source Han Sans）按各地区的字形点阵化，西文取 Source Sans 3 和 Noto，扩展区汉字取遍黑体。都是 SIL OFL 字体。Each region’s forms are rasterised from Source Han Sans; Latin from Source Sans 3 and Noto, rare hanzi from Plangothic. All are SIL OFL fonts.

  2. AI 逐字修整AI repair, glyph by glyph

    AI 按作者定下的画字规则修每一个字：同一个偏旁在所有字里逐像素一致；同一个字的各地区版本从一个母版派生，只改写法不同的笔画。部分汉字借鉴了 TsFreddie 手绘的圆石点阵黑。An AI repairs every glyph under drawing rules the author set: a component is pixel-identical in every character that uses it; a character’s regional versions derive from one master and change only the strokes that are written differently. Some hanzi borrow from TsFreddie’s hand-drawn TUMBLED.

  3. 作者审核Author review

    作者在编辑器里逐字审核、修改。通过的才标为“已审核”，程序和 AI 不能替作者通过。The author reviews and corrects each glyph in an editor. Only the author can mark a glyph approved; no script or AI can.
